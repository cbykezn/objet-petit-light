IDEA / 理念

拉岡在精神分析論中提出「objet petit a」，指向那個永遠無法完全抵達、卻持續驅動欲望的客體。 小幻光設計所 Objet Petit L(ighting)，懷抱著對美與意義的無盡渴求，致力於拓展光的敘事邊界，讓光不僅照亮世界，更觸動人心。

我們關注空間、物件與人的故事，透過實驗性的思維探索光影的可能性。意義不只存在於抵達終點，而在於持續追尋那些吸引我們向前的事物，小幻光以此打造能引發好奇、激起反思，充滿趣味與啟發性的光之體驗，為當代與未來開啟更多想像。

IDEA X IDEA

Objet Petit L(ighting) draws inspiration from Jacques Lacan’s concept of objet petit a—the unattainable object that perpetually fuels desire. We are a studio dedicated to expanding the narrative possibilities of light. Here, light is not merely a means of illumination, but a medium that resonates emotionally and reveals new ways of seeing.

Rooted in the stories that unfold between space, objects, and people, the studio approaches light through experimentation and curiosity. We believe that meaning is found not in arriving at a final destination, but in the continual pursuit of what draws us forward. Guided by this belief, we create lighting experiences that spark curiosity, invite reflection, and open new ways of imagining the present and the future.
